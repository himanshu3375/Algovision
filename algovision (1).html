About AlgoVision

AlgoVision is a visual learning platform designed to make Algorithms and Data Structures easier to understand through interactive animation and hands-on visualization.

Mission

Replace static diagrams and dry pseudocode with algorithms you can actually watch run, so intuition builds before memorization does.

Features

  • Interactive visualizations for sorting and searching
  • Step-by-step execution with play, pause, and single-step control
  • Real-time comparison and swap counts as each algorithm runs
  • A searchable, filterable catalog of algorithms

Benefits

  • See exactly why an algorithm behaves the way it does, not just what it outputs
  • Learn at your own pace — slow the animation down or step through by hand
  • Check understanding right away with the built-in quiz
  • No installs or accounts — it runs entirely in your browser

Technology

  • HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Real, correct sort and search implementations — every step shown is genuinely executing
  • No backend; theme and progress are saved locally in your browser
